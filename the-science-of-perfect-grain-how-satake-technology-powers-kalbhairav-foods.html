When it comes to rice, quality isn’t just grown in the fields—it is perfected in the mill. For the modern, health-conscious consumer, a bag of rice should offer more than just sustenance. It must deliver consistent texture, pristine cleanliness, and maximum nutritional retention.

At Kalbhairav Foods, we have always believed that traditional agriculture deserves modern technological excellence. That is why we are proud to announce that our entire facility is powered by 100% Satake Rice Milling Technology—the global gold standard in grain processing.

From local favorites to premium varieties, every single grain of rice that leaves our facility is processed using Satake’s world-renowned, Japanese-engineered machinery.

Who is Satake? The Global Pioneers of Grain Processing

Founded in Japan over a century ago, Satake engineered the world’s first power-driven rice milling machine. Today, they are the undisputed global leaders in grain processing technology. Satake is synonymous with precision, innovation, and an uncompromising commitment to food safety.

By integrating a complete, end-to-end Satake setup, Kalbhairav Foods brings this world-class Japanese engineering directly to your dinner table.

Inside the Satake Process: How We Mill Our Rice

Milling rice is a delicate science. If you apply too much friction, the grains break, leading to sticky, unevenly cooked rice. If you mill too aggressively, you strip away vital nutrients. Satake’s technology solves this through a highly sophisticated, multi-stage process:

1. Advanced Cleaning & De-stoning

Before milling even begins, raw paddy passes through Satake’s high-efficiency cleaning systems. This process gently removes all impurities—such as dust, straw, stones, and micro-particles—ensuring that only 100% pure paddy moves forward.

2. Precision De-husking (Husking)

Satake’s rubber roll huskers use automated, computerized tension controls to separate the outer husk from the brown rice. Because the machine adjusts dynamically to the size of the grain, it minimizes grain stress, drastically reducing breakage and keeping the kernel perfectly intact.

3. Gentle Whitening and Polishing

This is where the magic happens. Satake’s vertical and horizontal whitening machines gently remove the bran layer. Unlike conventional mills that generate high heat—which destroys nutrients and cracks the grain—Satake’s cool-milling technology keeps temperatures low. The subsequent polishing stage uses a fine water mist to give the rice its signature translucent, glossy finish without compromising its structural integrity.

4. Smart Grading and Sizing

A premium dining experience requires uniformity. Satake’s precision sizers separate broken or under-sized grains from the premium, full-length head rice. When you open a packet of Kalbhairav Foods rice, you will find uniformly sized grains that cook evenly every single time.

5. AI-Powered Optical Sorting

The final frontier of quality control is Satake’s state-of-the-art optical color sorter. Using high-definition cameras and advanced AI sensors, this machine scans every single grain of rice in milliseconds. Any grain with discoloration, chalkiness, or slight defects is instantly rejected via micro-jets of air. What remains is flawless, pristine rice.

Why Satake Technology Matters to You

By investing in a full Satake technological ecosystem, Kalbhairav Foods delivers distinct advantages to our consumers, distributors, and partners:

  1. Unmatched Hygiene & Purity: The entire automated process minimizes human contact, ensuring the highest standards of food safety and zero contamination.
  2. Superior Cooking Quality: Because our grains are uniformly sized and free from micro-cracks, they absorb water evenly. This results in fluffy, non-sticky, and aromatic rice whenever you cook.
  3. Enhanced Shelf Life: Satake’s precise polishing removes just the right amount of oil-rich bran, preventing the rice from turning rancid and significantly extending its natural shelf life.
  4. Nutritional Preservation: Our gentle milling process protects the essential nutrients locked within the grain, ensuring your meals are as healthy as they are delicious.

 

A Commitment to Excellence, One Grain at a Time

At Kalbhairav Foods, our journey begins in the finest paddy fields, but our promise is fulfilled through technological perfection. Our investment in full Satake technology reflects our core mission: never to compromise on the quality of food that feeds your family.

Whether you are cooking a simple daily meal, a fragrant biryani, or a traditional feast, you can taste the Japanese precision and Indian heritage blended into every grain of Kalbhairav Foods rice.
