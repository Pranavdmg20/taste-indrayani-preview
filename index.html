About Taste indrayani

Indrayani Rice is a cherished indigenous variety grown in the fertile Western Ghats of Maharashtra. Known for its naturally sticky texture, rich aroma and soft mouthfeel, it has been a staple in traditional Maharashtrian households for generations. Unlike highly polished rice, Indrayani is semi-polished, allowing it to retain more natural nutrients and fiber. It is easy to digest, comforting and ideal for everyday meals as well as festive preparations. Cultivated in mineral-rich soil and nourished by pure rainwater, Indrayani Rice represents authenticity, regional heritage and wholesome nutrition in every grain.
